# Japan details: the M2e checklist

What real Japanese streets and konbini are made of, and how they look after
years of use. It is the checklist every spot is judged against (SPEC M2e):
a spot lists which of these it shows and which it lacks.

Sources: the real Lawson photo (`real-day.png`), Tan's walks in
`reference/japan/` (Yotsugi, Tokyo), the density frames, and the web
sources listed at the end. Research only: no image from these is copied
into the game.

**Rendering rule:** real in content, painted in rendering. Every entry is
drawn as crisp painted shapes: flat tones, a few value steps, clean edges,
never photographic noise.

Status: **have** (in the town now), **weak** (present but short of the
entry), **missing**.
