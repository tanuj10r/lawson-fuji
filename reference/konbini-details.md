# Konbini details: the M3 checklist

How a real Lawson is laid out and furnished inside, and where each item goes
in ours. Every interior spot is judged against this list (SPEC M3). Research
only: no image from any source is copied into the game.

Rendering rule, as in japan-details.md: **real in content, painted in
rendering**. Flat, bright, crisp shapes. A konbini is lit evenly from a
ceiling full of lights, so the interior is painted, not lit by the sun.

Status: **have** / **weak** / **missing** (updated as M3 builds).

---

## 1. The plan of the room

| Detail | Real | Ours (17 × 10 m box, door left of centre) | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sight lines | Fixtures are low at the front and high at the back; from the door you see across to the drinks | Gondolas 1.5 m, below eye height (1.6 m); the cooler wall stands full height at the back | have |
| Destination goods at the back | Drinks and bento sit at the far end, on purpose: you walk past everything else | The walk-in cooler across the back wall; the open chilled case down the left wall | have |
| Register offset from the door | The register never faces the door head on | The counter runs down the right side, near the door (SPEC 5) | have |
| Loop | People walk the walls counter-clockwise | A cross aisle inside the door, a back aisle before the cooler, 1.4 m aisles between gondolas | have |

## 6. Sound (for M4)

- Our own original door chime (never the real entry jingle, AGENTS.md).
- The fridge hum, the ding of the register, the in-store BGM (ours), and the
  staff's いらっしゃいませ (M5).

## 7. Branding

The Lawson name only on the store, signs, uniform, receipt and bags
(AGENTS.md). Inside, that means the uniform and the bags at checkout.
Everything on the shelves is generic and ours; the ticket kiosk, coffee and
hot snacks are unnamed.
